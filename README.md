## About

### Learning is hard to keep up

Many adults want to study again, but long sessions are hard to keep going. Sprout 5 makes it small and fun.

- Made for adults who want to learn again
- Helps you build a daily study habit
- Your learning partner grows as you learn
- Soft forest-green look that feels calm

### How Sprout 5 works

Pick a topic, start the 5-minute timer, and finish. Your partner gets stronger every time you do.

- Main action: a 5-minute study timer
- Type a topic or tap a quick topic chip
- Growth stages: Seed, Sprout, Tree, Flower
- Log shows streak, total minutes, and notes

### Why it matters and next

Five minutes is easy to start, so it is easy to repeat. Small steps add up to a real habit.

- Seeing your partner grow keeps you going
- Streak days and one-line notes are in the app
- Next: decide the last small touch
- Choose between streak focus and note focus
